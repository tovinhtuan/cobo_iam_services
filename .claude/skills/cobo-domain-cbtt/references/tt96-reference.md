# TT96/2020/TT-BTC reference for Cobo (researched 2026-10)

Status: TT96 is in force, amended by TT68/2024/TT-BTC (from 02/11/2024:
Điều 5 language, Điều 25 k8, Điều 33), TT18/2025 and TT08/2026 (Điều 25 k8,
Phụ lục XVII - securities companies; no change found to issuer deadlines).
A draft circular to replace TT96 (annual report 120 days, governance report
30 days after FY end) was under consultation - NOT issued as of research date.

Primary text: https://vbpl.vn/FileData/TW/Lists/VBPQ/Attachments/146048/VanBanGoc_96-2020-TT-BTC.pdf

## Categories
- Định kỳ: Điều 10 (all public companies), Điều 14 (listed / large public companies).
- Bất thường: Điều 11 - within 24 hours of the event.
- Theo yêu cầu: Điều 12 - within 24 hours of SSC/exchange request.
- Hoạt động khác: Điều 13 - offering, issuance, listing, use of proceeds,
  foreign ownership limit, treasury shares (buyback reducing assets >10%:
  notify creditors and disclose within 15 days).

## Periodic deadlines
| Item | Basis | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Audited annual FS | Đ10 k1(c); Đ14 k1 | 10 days after audit report signed; max 90 days after FY end |
| Semi-annual reviewed FS | Đ14 k2(c) | 5 days after review report signed; max 45 days after half-year; parent company max 60 days |
| Quarterly FS | Đ14 k3(c) | 20 days after quarter end; parent company 30 days; if reviewed: 5 days after signing, max 45 days |
| Annual report (Phụ lục IV) | Đ10 k2 | 20 days after audited annual FS published; max 110 days after FY end |
| Corporate governance report (Phụ lục V) | Đ10 k4 | 30 days after end of first 6 months and of the year |
| AGM documents | Đ10 k3(a) | ≥21 days before opening (longer if charter requires) |
| AGM minutes / resolutions | Đ10 k3(b) → Đ11 k1(c) | 24 hours |

## Extraordinary events (Điều 11 khoản 1, main items)
Bank account frozen; state decisions / business suspension / registration
change; shareholder meeting decisions; treasury shares and securities
issuance; dividends, splits; restructuring, dissolution, charter changes;
accounting policy or auditor change; capital into/out of subsidiaries;
related-party / insider transactions; voting share count change; insider
personnel changes; asset transactions >15% of total assets; prosecution of
company or insiders; final court judgments, tax penalties; bankruptcy filing
accepted; other price-sensitive events; foreign listing/delisting.

## Responsibility and channels
- Đ6 k1: legal representative or one authorized disclosure person; fallback
  most senior executive; change reported within 24h.
- Đ7 k1: company website, SSC IDS, exchange; Đ4 k3: send to SSC and exchange
  simultaneously.
- Đ7 k2: website set up at registration as public company; address reported
  within 3 working days; investor relations section.
- Đ4 k5: periodic info stored ≥10 years, online ≥5 years; extraordinary
  info online ≥5 years.

## Language (Điều 5 as replaced by TT68/2024)
| Company type | Periodic in English from | Extraordinary/on-request/other from |
|---|---|---|
| Listed and large public | 01/01/2025 | 01/01/2026 |
| Other public | 01/01/2027 | 01/01/2028 |
Vietnamese version prevails on conflict.

## Day counting
- Periodic deadlines use "ngày" (calendar days). 24h items: hours.
- Working days only in specific clauses (website address 3 working days;
  insider trade plan ≥3 working days before trading under TT68).
- Đ7 k7-8: obligation arising on a rest day/holiday → post on own website,
  complete disclosure next working day.
- Đ3 k5 disclosure date = first appearance on any channel; Đ3 k6 report date
  = receipt by SSC/exchange.
- Civil Code 2015 Đ147-148 (default per Đ145): period starts the day after
  the event, ends 24:00 of last day; if last day is a rest day, ends at end of
  next working day. Application to TT96 periodic deadlines is an inference.

## Penalties - NĐ 156/2020/NĐ-CP Điều 42 (organizations; individuals 1/2, Điều 5)
| Fine (VND) | Violation |
|---|---|
| 10-30M | Wrong means/form, not retaining disclosed info |
| 50-70M | Late disclosure (k3(a)), incomplete disclosure |
| 70-100M | Non-disclosure (k4(a)), not confirming/correcting on time |
| 100-200M | False/misleading info, concealment |
Uncertain: exact khoản for 10-30M and 100-200M bands; NĐ 128/2021 and
NĐ 306/2025 (in force 09/01/2026) amendments to Điều 42 - verify.
